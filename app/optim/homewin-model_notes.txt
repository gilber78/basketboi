Quadratic fit doesn't make sense for our purposes. We are either okay with different behavior at the extremes of the data or not.
There's not really a situation where the probability of winning goes *up* at the extremes, it's always an odd function.
5th degree doesn't make sense either, since there's usually only 0 or 2 critical points.
This limits us to linear or cubic fits. We'll keep plotting quadratic to make it clear when linear is the better fit, since all 3 will be extremely close in value.

Based on the R^2 sin^2(2 * arctan(m)) fitness scores, 0.17594335277687914 (Fig28) is the smallest fitness currently used.
Considering the low strength of signal scores for all 16 X? parameters, and the fact that they are dependent on the home team winning, i'ts not a good idea to use them for homewin
Save this for a split spread/total model that takes the form p * M1 + (1 - p) * M2
